## 1. Layer Architecture

The framework is split into three strictly separated layers, each with a defined interface to the next. **Only Layer I is claimed to be physical.**

### Layer I — Physical Core (Correlation Substrate)
- Non-commutative correlation algebra
- Emergent spacetime, QFT, gauge structure, fermion content
- Dark sector, gravity, quantum-gravity regularization
- **Status:** Mathematical conjecture. Not yet derived.

### Layer II — Observer / Inference Layer
- Holographic inference mapping \(R = \tanh(WC+S)\)
- Coherence polytope for bounded inference systems
- **Status:** Phenomenological model of information-processing systems. Applies to any bounded inference agent, biological or artificial.
- **Interface to Layer I:** Layer II is an *effective description* of a subsystem of Layer I; the reduction map is **[OPEN]**.

### Layer III — Interpretive / Normative Layer (Quarantined)
- Cognitive axes (P–B–T–p), pathology/optimization taxonomy
- Maximal-coherence attractor, Buddhist siddhi mapping, Nirvana-as-REDEEMED
- **Status:** **Analogical and normative only.** No physical claim. Cannot be used to resolve open problems in Layer I.
- **Interface to Layer II:** Descriptive correspondence only. No derivation claimed.

**Rule:** Any statement in Layer III that appears to resolve a Layer I problem is a **category error** and is prohibited in this document.

## 12. Closing Statement

CHC-TOE v0.2 is a **deliberately deflated** blueprint. It retains the ambition of v0.1 — a correlation-substrate unification with an observer layer — but strips the interpretive and teleological superstructure from the physical core, re-opens every "resolved" gap, and converts claims into derivation tasks with acceptance criteria.

The framework's value now depends on whether **D1–D5** can be completed and whether any of **D6–D21** yields a non-trivial result. Until then, it is a research program, not a theory.
